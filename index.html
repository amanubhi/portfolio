The American Affordability Index

01

Python, pandas, DuckDB SQL, Streamlit, Plotly

Did income keep pace with the cost of living? I brought together Census, BLS, and EIA data to compare renter income with selected essentials, investigate state housing costs, and build an interactive budget simulator.

A reproducible pipeline connects source snapshots, validation checks, analysis-ready tables, SQL, and a public dashboard. The analysis shows which assumptions drive the result, not just the headline.

The model covers selected costs using illustrative budgets and aggregate renter income. It does not measure every household’s full living costs or predict future affordability.

A way to compare salaries across 32,672 job postings. Choose a role, country, and employment type; the workbook updates the charts and summary figures.
